# Company FAQ

Short answers to questions new employees often ask.

## What does Acme Robotics make?

Autonomous mobile robots that move goods inside warehouses and hospitals, plus the fleet control software that coordinates them.

## How big is the company?

About 420 employees across the Austin, Boston and Berlin offices and remote locations.

## When was it founded?

Acme Robotics was founded in 2017 in Austin, Texas.

## Is there a dress code?

No. Wear what you are comfortable in. Wear closed-toe shoes in the hardware lab and at customer sites.

## Can I bring my dog to work?

Dogs are welcome in the Austin office on Fridays, if they are friendly and house-trained. Dogs are not allowed in the hardware lab.

## Where do I find the org chart?

The org chart is in the HR portal under People.
