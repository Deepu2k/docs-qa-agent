# Meeting Guidelines

Meetings are expensive. These guidelines help keep them useful.

## Focus Fridays

Fridays are meeting-free for everyone except customer-facing teams. Use the time for focused work.

## Agendas

Every meeting longer than 15 minutes needs an agenda in the invitation. If there is no agenda, you can decline.

## Default lengths

Calendar invitations default to 25 minutes instead of 30, and 50 minutes instead of 60, so people have time between meetings.

## Recording

Record meetings that include people in other time zones, and announce the recording at the start. Recordings are deleted automatically after 90 days.

## All-hands

The company all-hands meeting happens on the first Thursday of every month at 11am Central Time.
