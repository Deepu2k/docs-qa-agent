# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

Acme Robotics believes rested people do better work. This policy explains how paid time off is earned, requested and tracked. It applies to all employees in the United States. Employees in Germany follow the Berlin addendum maintained by the People team.

## Who is eligible

All full-time employees are eligible for PTO from their first day. Part-time employees who are scheduled for at least 20 hours per week are also eligible. Contractors, interns on fixed-term agreements and temporary staff are not covered by this policy; their time off is defined in their individual agreements.

## How PTO accrues

Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of PTO per month, which adds up to 18 days per year. Accrual happens on the last day of each month, so an employee who starts on March 10 receives their first 1.5 days on March 31. Part-time employees accrue PTO pro rata based on their scheduled hours; for example, an employee scheduled for 30 hours per week accrues 75 percent of the full-time rate.

After five full years of service, the accrual rate increases to 2 days per month, or 24 days per year. The increase takes effect on the first day of the month after the employee's fifth work anniversary.

## Carryover and balance limits

Up to 5 unused PTO days carry over into the next calendar year. Any balance above 5 days is forfeited on January 1. There is no payout for forfeited days. Employees can see their current balance and projected year-end balance at any time in the HR portal under Time Off.

Your balance can never go below zero. If you need time off before you have accrued enough PTO, talk to your manager about unpaid leave.

## Requesting time off

Submit every PTO request in the HR portal, even for a single day. For absences longer than 3 consecutive working days, submit the request at least 2 weeks in advance. Your manager approves or declines within 3 business days. If your manager has not responded after 3 business days, the request escalates automatically to your skip-level manager.

Requests during the last two weeks of December are approved first-come, first-served to keep each team at least half staffed.

## Using PTO while traveling

You can combine PTO with a business trip, for example by staying through a weekend. The company pays only the costs it would have paid for the business portion of the trip. See the travel booking policy for how to book these trips.

## Payout when you leave

Accrued but unused PTO is paid out in your final paycheck when you leave Acme Robotics, up to a maximum of 10 days.
