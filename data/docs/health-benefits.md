# Health Benefits

Acme Robotics offers medical, dental and vision coverage to all full-time employees and to part-time employees scheduled for at least 30 hours per week. You can cover a spouse or domestic partner and children up to age 26.

## Medical plans

There are two medical plans, both administered by Blue Cross.

The PPO plan has a $500 individual deductible and a $1,000 family deductible. Acme pays 100 percent of the employee premium and 80 percent of the premium for dependents. You can see any in-network doctor without a referral.

The HDHP plan, a high-deductible health plan, has a $2,000 individual deductible and a $4,000 family deductible. Acme pays 100 percent of the premium for employees and dependents. The HDHP is paired with a health savings account (HSA).

## Health savings account

If you choose the HDHP, Acme contributes $1,000 per year to your HSA for individual coverage, or $2,000 per year for family coverage. The contribution is split evenly across paychecks. You can add your own pre-tax contributions up to the IRS limit.

## Dental and vision

Dental coverage is through Delta Dental and includes two cleanings per year at no cost and orthodontics for children. Vision coverage through VSP includes one eye exam per year and a $200 allowance for glasses or contact lenses every 12 months. Acme pays the full premium for dental and vision for employees and dependents.

## Mental health

Every employee and dependent receives 12 free therapy sessions per year through Lyra, in addition to medical plan coverage. Sessions are confidential and are not reported to Acme.

## Enrolling and making changes

New hires enroll within 30 days of their start date; see the onboarding guide. Everyone else can change plans only during open enrollment in November, or within 30 days of a qualifying life event such as marriage, birth or adoption, or loss of other coverage.

## Life and disability insurance

Acme provides life insurance equal to two times your annual salary, and short-term and long-term disability insurance, at no cost to you.
