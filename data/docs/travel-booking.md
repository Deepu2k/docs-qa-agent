# Travel Booking Policy

All business travel is booked through Navan, the company travel tool, so that we can find you in an emergency and keep costs predictable. Meal limits and how to submit travel expenses are covered in the expense policy.

## Before you book

Business travel needs manager approval before booking. International travel also needs director approval. Book at least 14 days in advance whenever possible; fares booked within 7 days of departure are reviewed by finance.

## Flights

Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Flights of 6 hours or more, measured as scheduled flying time for a single leg, may be booked in premium economy. Business class is allowed only for flights over 10 hours and only with vice president approval.

Choose the lowest fare within two hours of your preferred departure time. You may keep frequent flyer miles, but loyalty programs must not influence which airline you choose. One checked bag is reimbursable each way.

## Hotels

Hotels are capped at $250 per night, plus taxes. In New York, San Francisco and London the cap is $350 per night. If a conference hotel costs more than the cap, you may book it with manager approval.

Book through Navan so the rate is applied automatically. If you stay with friends or family instead of a hotel, you may expense a thank-you gift up to $50 per trip.

## Trains and cars

Trains are encouraged for trips under 4 hours. Book standard class. Rental cars are allowed when they are cheaper than taxis or when no other transport exists; choose a compact or midsize car and decline the rental company's insurance, which our corporate policy already covers.

## Combining business and personal travel

You may extend a business trip for personal reasons. Book the extra nights yourself and pay for them personally. If the extension changes your flight price, you pay the difference.

## Travel safety

Navan sends your itinerary to the security team. If you are traveling to a country on the high-risk list, the security team will contact you before departure with a briefing. In an emergency while traveling, call the 24-hour travel assistance line printed on the back of your corporate card.
