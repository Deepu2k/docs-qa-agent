# On-Call

Engineers on product and infrastructure teams join their team's on-call rotation after their first 3 months.

## Rotation

Each shift lasts one week, from Monday 10am to the following Monday 10am. A primary and a secondary engineer are on call. The secondary is paged if the primary does not acknowledge a page within 10 minutes.

## Expectations

Acknowledge pages within 10 minutes and stay within reach of your laptop and a reliable internet connection. If you need to step away for a few hours, ask the secondary to cover.

## On-call pay

Engineers receive $200 for each week they are primary on call, and $100 for each week as secondary. If you are paged outside working hours, you may take time off in lieu the next day.

## Handoff

At the end of a shift, write a short handoff note in the team channel listing open issues and anything unusual.
