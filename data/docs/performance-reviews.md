# Performance Reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each cycle takes about three weeks from self-review to the final conversation with your manager.

## The cycle

1. Self-review: you write a short summary of your impact and growth.
2. Peer feedback: you choose 3 to 5 peers, and your manager may add others.
3. Manager review: your manager writes an assessment and proposes a rating.
4. Calibration: managers in each department meet to make ratings consistent across teams.
5. Conversation: your manager shares the written review and rating in a 1:1.

## Ratings

There are four ratings: Exceeds, Strong, Developing and Not meeting. There is no forced distribution, but calibration checks that ratings mean the same thing across teams.

## Promotions

Promotions are decided during the April cycle. Your manager writes a promotion case, which is reviewed by a committee of senior leaders in your department. You cannot be promoted in October except to fix a clear error.

## Disagreeing with your review

If you disagree with your rating, add a written response in the HR portal within 14 days of your review conversation. Your skip-level manager reads every response.
