# Parental Leave

Acme Robotics offers paid leave to all employees welcoming a new child through birth, adoption or foster placement.

## How much leave

Birthing parents receive 20 weeks of fully paid leave. Non-birthing parents, including adoptive and foster parents, receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave. You become eligible after 3 months of employment.

## When to take it

Leave can be taken all at once or in up to three blocks, all within 12 months of the birth or placement.

## Returning to work

Employees returning from parental leave can work a reduced 4-day week at full pay for their first 4 weeks back.

## Requesting leave

Tell the People team at least 30 days before your expected leave start date, if possible.
