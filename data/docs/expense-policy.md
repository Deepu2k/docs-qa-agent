# Expense Policy

This policy covers reimbursable business expenses for all Acme Robotics employees. The goal is simple: spend company money as if it were your own, and never be out of pocket for legitimate business costs. Travel booking rules, including flight class and hotel limits, live in the separate travel booking policy.

## General principles

An expense is reimbursable if it is necessary for your work, reasonable in amount, and documented. When in doubt, ask your manager before you spend. Managers approve expenses for their direct reports; directors approve anything a manager submits for themselves. Splitting a purchase into several smaller ones to avoid an approval limit is a policy violation.

## Approval limits

Any single purchase over $1,000 requires written approval from your manager before you buy it. Purchases over $5,000 require approval from a vice president and should usually go through procurement instead of an expense report.

## Meals

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $75 per day, including tips. This daily limit covers breakfast, lunch and dinner together, and it does not apply on days when meals are provided by a conference or hotel. Team meals at your home office are not reimbursable unless they are approved in advance as a team event.

Client dinners may exceed the daily limit with director approval. List every attendee and their company on the expense report.

Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances, including at client dinners. If a bill includes alcohol, deduct it before submitting.

## Ground transportation

Taxis and rideshares are reimbursable when traveling for business or when working past 9pm at the office. Use the standard ride option rather than premium options. If you drive your own car for business, mileage is reimbursed at 70 cents per mile; commuting between your home and your usual office is not reimbursable. Parking and tolls on business trips are reimbursable.

## Phones and internet

Employees who use a personal phone for work receive a $40 monthly phone stipend. Internet costs for remote and hybrid employees are covered by the internet stipend described in the remote work policy, not through expense reports.

## Gifts and entertainment

Gifts to clients are limited to $100 per person per year. Gifts to government officials are not allowed at all. Entertainment such as sporting events requires director approval in advance.

## What is never reimbursable

The following are never reimbursed: alcohol, personal entertainment such as in-flight movies, traffic and parking fines, upgrades you choose for comfort, lost baggage fees caused by your own delay, and expenses for a spouse or guest.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expenses in the expense tool within 30 days of the purchase date. Attach an itemized receipt for any item over $25; a credit card statement is not an itemized receipt. Expenses submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed.

Approved reimbursements are paid in the next payroll cycle by direct deposit. If your report is rejected, you will get an email explaining why, and you can fix and resubmit it.

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than four times per year can request a corporate card from finance. Corporate card charges still need receipts and must be reconciled in the expense tool within 30 days. Never use the corporate card for personal purchases, even if you intend to pay it back.
