# IT Support

## Getting help

Open a ticket in the IT portal, or email it-help@acme-robotics.example. The IT helpdesk is staffed from 7am to 7pm Central Time on weekdays. Urgent issues outside those hours, such as being locked out before a customer meeting, can be reported by phone at extension 4357.

## Response times

Urgent tickets get a first response within 1 hour during staffed hours. Standard tickets get a first response within 1 business day.

## Software requests

Request software that is not in the app catalog through the IT portal. IT and security review new tools within 5 business days.

## Accounts

Account access is managed through single sign-on. Ask your manager to request access to team-specific tools; IT will not grant access without manager approval.
