# Acceptable Use of Company Systems

Company laptops, accounts and networks are provided for work. Limited personal use, such as checking personal email at lunch, is fine as long as it does not interfere with your work or break the rules below.

## Not allowed

- Using company systems for illegal activity or harassment
- Mining cryptocurrency on company hardware or cloud accounts
- Running personal businesses or side projects on company infrastructure
- Sharing confidential information on social media or public forums

## AI tools

You may use company-approved AI assistants for work. Do not paste customer data, credentials or unreleased product plans into AI tools that are not on the approved list in the app catalog.

## Monitoring

Company systems are monitored for security purposes. Do not store anything on a company device that you would not want the security team to see.
