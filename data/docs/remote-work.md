# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Acme Robotics is a hybrid company. This policy explains where and when you work.

## Office days

Employees who live within 50 miles of an Acme office work on-site on Tuesdays and Wednesdays. These anchor days exist so teams can plan in-person collaboration. Everyone else is fully remote and is invited to the office for quarterly team weeks, with travel paid under the travel booking policy.

## Core hours

Core collaboration hours are 10am to 3pm in your team's home time zone. Meetings should be scheduled within core hours. Outside core hours you can arrange your schedule however suits you, as long as your work gets done.

## Working from another country

You may work from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year. Request approval from HR at least 4 weeks before you travel, because some countries create tax or visa obligations even for short stays. Working from a country under US sanctions is never permitted.

## Moving to a new state or country

If you plan to move permanently, tell the People team at least 60 days in advance. Your pay may be adjusted to the location band of your new home.

## Internet stipend

Remote and hybrid employees receive a $50 monthly internet stipend, paid automatically with payroll. You do not need to submit receipts.

## Coworking spaces

Fully remote employees may expense a coworking membership of up to $300 per month.
