# Data Retention

We keep data only as long as we need it.

## Retention periods

- Email: 3 years, then deleted automatically
- Chat messages: 18 months
- Meeting recordings: 90 days
- Robot telemetry from customer fleets: 1 year, unless the customer contract says otherwise
- Financial and payroll records: 7 years, as required by law
- Candidate data for people we did not hire: 2 years

## Legal holds

If legal places a hold on data related to a dispute, it must not be deleted until the hold is lifted, even if its retention period has passed. Legal will tell you directly if you are affected.

## Customer deletion requests

When a customer asks us to delete their data, support opens a deletion ticket. The data must be deleted within 30 days.
