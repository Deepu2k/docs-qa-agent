# Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Acme Robotics. This guide covers your first weeks.

## Before your first day

Laptops ship to remote employees 5 business days before the start date. If your laptop has not arrived 2 days before you start, email it-help@acme-robotics.example.

## First day

On your first day you set up your laptop, enroll in MFA, and meet your onboarding buddy, an experienced teammate who answers questions during your first month. Your manager schedules a welcome meeting for 10am your time.

## First 30 days

Complete the security training within your first 7 days. Schedule 1:1 meetings with each teammate during your first 2 weeks. Engineers ship their first code change, however small, within their first 2 weeks.

## Benefits enrollment

You must enroll in health benefits within 30 days of your start date. Coverage begins on your start date once you enroll. If you miss the window you must wait for open enrollment in November, unless you have a qualifying life event.

## Probation and check-ins

There is no probation period. Your first performance check-in with your manager happens at 90 days.
