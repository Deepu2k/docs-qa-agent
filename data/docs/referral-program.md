# Employee Referral Program

Great people know great people. If you refer someone and we hire them, you receive a referral bonus.

## Bonus amounts

The referral bonus is $3,000 for engineering roles and $1,500 for all other roles. The bonus is paid after your referral has been employed for 90 days, in the next regular paycheck.

## Who is eligible

All employees can earn referral bonuses except recruiters and the hiring manager for the role. Referrals of former employees who left within the last 12 months do not qualify.

## How to refer someone

Submit the referral in the recruiting tool with the candidate's resume and a short note about how you know them. Tell the candidate first so they expect to hear from us. Referred candidates go through the same interview process as everyone else.
