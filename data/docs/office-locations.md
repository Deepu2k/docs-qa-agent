# Office Locations

Acme Robotics has three offices.

## Austin (headquarters)

500 Robotics Way, Austin, Texas. Open 7am to 8pm on weekdays. Free garage parking for employees. The Austin office has the main hardware lab.

## Boston

200 Harbor Street, Boston, Massachusetts. Open 8am to 7pm on weekdays. Parking is not provided; employees receive a monthly transit pass instead.

## Berlin

Kastanienallee 40, Berlin, Germany. Open 8am to 6pm on weekdays. Berlin employees follow local holidays and the Berlin addendum to the PTO policy.

## Visitors

Register visitors in the front desk system at least 1 day ahead. Visitors must be escorted at all times in the hardware lab.

## Badges

Your badge opens your home office and, with manager approval, any other office. Report a lost badge to the front desk on the same day.
