# Security Guidelines

Every employee is responsible for protecting company and customer data. Robots built by Acme run in warehouses and hospitals, so a security mistake can have physical consequences. These guidelines are the minimum; your team may add stricter rules.

## Passwords

Use the company password manager, 1Password, for all work accounts. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and unique to each account. Never reuse a work password for a personal account. Never share a password with anyone, including IT staff; IT will never ask for it.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required on email, the code repository, the HR portal, the expense tool and every production system. Use a hardware security key or the authenticator app; SMS codes are not accepted because they can be intercepted. If you lose your security key, IT can issue a temporary bypass code valid for 24 hours.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to security@acme-robotics.example within 1 hour of noticing. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device. Reporting quickly matters more than anything else; you will never be disciplined for losing a device, but you may be for reporting it late.

## Phishing

Forward suspicious emails to phishing@acme-robotics.example and do not click any links or open attachments. Signs of phishing include urgent requests for payment, unexpected password reset emails, and senders whose address does not match their display name. The security team runs a phishing simulation every quarter. Employees who click a simulated phishing link are enrolled in a 15-minute refresher course.

## Customer data

Customer data must never be copied to personal devices, personal email or personal cloud storage. Production data access requires a ticket approved by your manager and expires after 7 days. Data exports for customers must be encrypted and sent through the secure transfer portal.

## Locking your screen

Lock your screen whenever you step away from your laptop, even at home. Laptops lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.

## Software installation

Install software only from the company app catalog or the official vendor site. Browser extensions that read page content need security approval. Never disable the endpoint protection agent.

## Public networks

The VPN connects automatically on untrusted networks. Do not turn it off on public Wi-Fi in airports, hotels or cafes.

## Reporting a security concern

If you think something is wrong, report it. Post in the #security-help channel for questions, or email security@acme-robotics.example for anything urgent. The security team responds to urgent reports within 30 minutes, 24 hours a day.
