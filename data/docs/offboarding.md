# Offboarding

This guide covers what happens when someone leaves Acme Robotics.

## Notice

We ask employees to give at least 2 weeks' notice when resigning. Tell your manager first, then the People team will schedule an exit conversation.

## Returning equipment

Return your laptop and any IT-shipped equipment within 10 days of your last day. IT emails a prepaid shipping label. Equipment bought with your home office stipend is yours to keep.

## Final pay

Your final paycheck includes pay through your last day and payout of unused PTO, up to the limit in the PTO policy. Outstanding expenses must be submitted before your last day.

## Benefits after leaving

Health coverage continues until the end of the month in which you leave. After that you can continue coverage through COBRA at your own cost.

## Accounts

Your accounts are disabled at 5pm on your last day.
