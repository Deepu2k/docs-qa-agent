# Compensation and Payroll

## Pay schedule

US employees are paid semi-monthly, on the 15th and the last day of each month. If a payday falls on a weekend or holiday, you are paid on the previous business day. Pay stubs are available in the HR portal.

## Salary bands

Every role has a salary band based on level and location. Your level and band are visible to you in the HR portal. Bands are reviewed every year against market data.

## Raises

Merit raises take effect on May 1, after the April performance review cycle. Promotions come with a raise of at least 8 percent.

## Equity

Most employees receive stock options when they join, vesting over 4 years with a 1-year cliff. Refresh grants are considered during the April review cycle.

## Bonuses

There is no annual cash bonus for most roles. Sales roles have commission plans described in their individual plan documents.

## Changing your bank account or tax withholding

Update your direct deposit and tax withholding in the HR portal. Changes made at least 5 business days before payday apply to that payday.
