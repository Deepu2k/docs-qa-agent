# Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Use it when you, or a family member you care for, are ill or have a medical appointment.

## Sick days

Every employee receives 10 paid sick days per year. The full allowance is granted on January 1, or prorated on your start date if you join mid-year. Sick days do not carry over and are not paid out when you leave.

## Reporting an absence

Tell your manager as early as possible on the day, before your first meeting if you can. Then log the absence in the HR portal under Time Off, choosing the Sick type. You do not need to share details of your illness.

## Doctor's notes

A doctor's note is required only for absences of more than 3 consecutive working days. Upload the note to the HR portal; it is visible only to the People team, not to your manager.

## Extended illness

If you expect to be out for more than two weeks, contact the People team at people@acme-robotics.example to discuss short-term disability coverage.
