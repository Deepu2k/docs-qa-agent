# Equipment and Home Office

## Laptops

Every employee receives a company laptop. Engineers choose between a 16-inch MacBook Pro and a Linux laptop; everyone else receives a 14-inch MacBook Air by default. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. If your laptop breaks before then, IT replaces it.

## Home office stipend

New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500 for equipment such as monitors, chairs, desks and keyboards. After that, you receive $250 every two years to replace worn-out items. Submit purchases through the expense tool with the category Home Office.

Equipment bought with the stipend belongs to you and does not need to be returned when you leave. The laptop and any equipment shipped by IT belong to the company.

## Peripherals

IT stocks standard keyboards, mice, headsets and USB-C docks. Request them through the IT portal; they ship in about 3 business days.

## Personal devices

You may read email and chat on a personal phone if it is enrolled in the company device management app. Personal laptops may not be used for work.
