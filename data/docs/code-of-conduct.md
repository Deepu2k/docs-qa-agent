# Code of Conduct

We expect everyone at Acme Robotics to treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect.

## Expected behavior

Be honest, assume good intent, and disagree about ideas rather than people. Give credit to others for their work.

## Harassment and discrimination

Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated, whether in the office, online, at company events or on business trips.

## Conflicts of interest

Tell your manager and the legal team about any outside job, investment or relationship that could conflict with your work at Acme. Outside work for a competitor is not allowed.

## Reporting concerns

Report concerns to your manager, the People team, or the anonymous ethics hotline at 1-800-555-0142, which is run by an independent company and is available 24 hours a day. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is a serious violation of this code.
