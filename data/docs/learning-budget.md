# Learning and Development Budget

Every employee has a learning budget of $1,500 per calendar year for courses, books, certifications and conferences related to their work. Unused budget does not carry over.

## What it covers

Online courses, technical books, certification exam fees, and conference tickets are all eligible. Travel to an approved conference is paid from the travel budget, not your learning budget, as long as your manager approves the trip.

## How to use it

Get your manager's approval for anything over $200, then pay and submit the receipt in the expense tool under the category Learning. Books under $50 need no approval.

## Study time

You can spend up to 4 hours per week of work time on learning, with your manager's agreement.

## Degree programs

Degree programs are not covered by the learning budget. They are reviewed case by case by the People team.
